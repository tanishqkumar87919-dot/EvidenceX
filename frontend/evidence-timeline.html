Early assertions claimed a blanket 35% reduction in cardiovascular disease through exercise alone, without distinguishing workout volume or intensity. Studies like UAH (2018) raised methodological concerns regarding self-reported activity recall biases.

Claim Scope 150+ min/wk Moderate Aerobic
Consensus Level High Agreement (94%)

Rigorous meta-analyses (Cochrane 2021) and updated global guidelines (WHO 2022, AHA 2023) established that 30% to 35% risk reduction is mathematically reproducible specifically when meeting the 150-minute weekly threshold, establishing a qualified confirmation.

Temporal Consensus Shift: Over the 8-year span, scientific and journalistic consensus moved from skepticism about blanket statistical promises to robust empirical validation of the underlying causal link, conditioned upon consistent volume.
