Why was this claim supported?
Evidence-Grounded Synthesis 14:21:05 · Investigation INV-2026-0847

Claim 01 was determined to be Mostly Supported with an 88% confidence score because multiple independent clinical trials and public health bodies confirm that regular aerobic exercise reduces cardiovascular risk by up to 35%.

Specifically, the American Heart Association [SRC-01: AHA 2023] and the Cochrane Systematic Review [SRC-02: Cochrane 2021] document relative risk reductions between 30% and 35% across 28 randomized trials. The World Health Organization [SRC-03: WHO 2022] also incorporates this benchmark.

However, EvidenceX added a "mostly" qualification rather than declaring absolute verification because this 35% ceiling is contingent upon completing at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity weekly, rather than minimal casual movement.
