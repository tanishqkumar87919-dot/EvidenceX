Trust & Rigor

Evidence Architecture

EvidenceX operates under strict principles of epistemic humility, transparent sourcing, and lateral reading.

Lateral Source Reading

Instead of taking claims or websites at face value, EvidenceX cross-examines external consensus, institutional affiliations, and historical credibility.

Epistemic Humility

When evidence is insufficient or conflicting, EvidenceX explicitly assigns 'Insufficient Evidence' or 'Inconclusive' rather than guessing.

Explainable Reasoning

Every verdict is supported by human-readable reasoning traces with exact source citations, publication dates, and strength assessments.
