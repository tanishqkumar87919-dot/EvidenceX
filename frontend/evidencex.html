Process

How Verification Works

A structured four-stage pipeline turns raw input into an evidence-backed, explainable verdict.

01
Extract Claims
The system parses your input and isolates every discrete, verifiable factual assertion — separating claims from opinion and context.
02
Find Evidence
Each claim is researched across credible sources: academic databases, peer-reviewed journals, established news archives, and official publications.
03
Compare Sources
Supporting and contradicting evidence is weighed by source credibility, recency, and methodological strength — not just quantity.
04
Explain the Verdict
A clear, structured verdict is produced with traceable reasoning. Every conclusion links to the evidence that generated it.

Our Principles

Built on Trust

Information verification requires epistemic honesty. These principles are not marketing — they are design constraints.

Evidence First
Every verdict is derived from traceable, cited evidence — not model intuition. If the evidence is thin, the verdict reflects that uncertainty explicitly.
Transparent Reasoning
You can see exactly why a verdict was reached. Every inference step, source weight, and comparison is exposed — there are no black-box conclusions.
Uncertainty Aware
EvidenceX does not fabricate confidence. When evidence is genuinely mixed or insufficient, the system communicates that clearly rather than defaulting to a false verdict.
